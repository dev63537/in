Project Overview

What is InventMitra?

A Smart Way to Manage Inventory

InventMitra is a web-based system that helps businesses track products, manage suppliers, handle purchases and sales, and generate reports — all from one simple dashboard.

Built with PHP and MySQL, it replaces notebooks and spreadsheets with a fast, reliable digital solution.

Why We Built It

Many small businesses still track inventory on paper or spreadsheets. This leads to stock errors, lost data, and wasted time.

InventMitra brings everything online — making inventory management simple, fast, and error-free.

Who Will Use It

  • Small Business Owners
  • Warehouse Managers
  • Shop Keepers
  • Inventory Staff

How It Works

The Problem

Why Do We Need This System?

Step 1

Manual Data Entry

Recording stock levels on paper leads to human errors and illegible handwriting.

Step 2

Information Gap

Takes hours to find out exactly how much stock is left or when to reorder.

Solution

Digital Centralization

All inventory data stored securely in one place, updated in real-time.

The Core Issue

Without a centralized system, businesses face constant stockouts of popular items and overstocking of slow-moving items, directly impacting their profitability and customer satisfaction.

Project Objectives

What We Aim to Achieve

Centralized Inventory Control

To develop a robust web application that serves as a single source of truth for all inventory operations, seamlessly connecting products, suppliers, purchases, and sales in one unified platform.

Enhance Data Security

Implement role-based access control to ensure sensitive financial and inventory data is only accessible to authorized personnel.

Real-Time Processing

Enable instant updates to stock levels whenever a purchase is received or a sale is dispatched, eliminating delays.

Automated Reporting

Generate comprehensive reports for sales, purchases, and current stock status with a single click.

Time Efficiency

Dramatically reduce the administrative hours spent on manual counting and data entry.

Core Modules

The system is divided into two primary modules based on user roles and responsibilities.

Admin Module

  • Full access to dashboard and analytics
  • Manage Staff accounts (Add/Edit/Delete)
  • Manage Suppliers and Categories
  • Generate complete system reports

Staff Module

  • View assigned dashboard summary
  • Manage day-to-day Products
  • Record incoming Purchases (Stock In)
  • Record outgoing Sales (Stock Out)
